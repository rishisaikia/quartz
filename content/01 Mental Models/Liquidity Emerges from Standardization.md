## Definition

Markets become liquid when *assets are comparable, processes repeatable, and outcomes predictable.*

## Key Insight

Uniqueness kills liquidity. Standardization enables faster matching between buyers and sellers.

## How to Apply

For positive outcomes:

- Normalize unit specifications and disclosure formats
    
- Standardize legal, financial, and execution steps
    
- Use repeatable auction formats across inventory types
    

For negative outcomes:

- Treat every transaction as bespoke
    
- Allow non-standard terms per buyer or seller
    
- Vary documentation and timelines arbitrarily
    

## Real-World Examples

- Equity markets with standardized lots
    
- Bond markets with rating frameworks
    
- REITs versus individual property sales
    

## Common Pitfalls

- Over-standardizing genuinely unique assets
    
- Ignoring local regulatory variations
    
- Confusing standardization with commoditization
