## Definition
The tendency to let one positive trait (halo) or negative trait (horns) color your overall impression of a person, product, or organization. One characteristic influences how you perceive everything else about them.

## Key Insight
We don't evaluate people or things on independent criteria—we let one strong impression bleed into all other judgments. Attractive people seem smarter and more trustworthy. Successful companies seem well-managed in every way. One bad experience makes everything else seem worse.

## How to Apply
Combat these biases by:
- Evaluating different attributes independently
- Deliberately seeking contradictory information
- Asking "Am I assuming X is good at everything because they're good at one thing?"
- Writing down assessments separately before forming overall opinion

## Real-World Example
Halo Effect: A well-dressed, articulate candidate interviews for a job. Their presentation skills create a halo effect—you assume they're also competent, hardworking, and intelligent, even though you have no evidence of these qualities. You might overlook red flags because the positive first impression colors everything.

Horns Effect: A colleague is late to one meeting and seems disorganized. Now you perceive all their work as sloppy, even when it's actually good. The one negative trait (tardiness) creates a horns effect that makes you see incompetence everywhere.

Product reviews: One exceptionally good feature (beautiful design) makes people rate everything else higher—battery life, performance, value. One bad feature (poor customer service) makes people rate product quality lower, even though it's unrelated.

Attractive people are judged as more competent in job interviews, receive lighter criminal sentences, and are assumed to be more trustworthy—all halo effects from physical appearance bleeding into unrelated judgments.

## Common Pitfalls
- Thinking you're immune to these effects
- Overcorrecting and being unfairly harsh on people who made good first impressions
- Not recognizing when others are experiencing halo/horns effects about you
