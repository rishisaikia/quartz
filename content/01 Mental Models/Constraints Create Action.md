## Definition

Well-designed limits on choice, time, and behavior increase the likelihood of decisive action.

## Key Insight

Freedom without boundaries produces inertia. Constraints reduce cognitive load and force movement.

## How to Apply

For positive outcomes:

- Require bid bonds or participation deposits
    
- Limit number of bids or bid revisions
    
- Enforce strict entry and exit rules
    

For negative outcomes:

- Allow unlimited browsing without commitment
    
- Permit casual, non-binding offers
    
- Keep participation costless and reversible
    

## Real-World Examples

- Non-refundable application fees
    
- Limited-seat exams or certifications
    
- Earnest money in real estate contracts
    

## Common Pitfalls

- Excessive constraints that deter participation
    
- Asymmetric constraints favoring one side
    
- Constraints introduced too late in the funnel
