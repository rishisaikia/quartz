## Definition

Trust is shifted from individual actors to transparent, rule-based systems that behave predictably regardless of who operates them.

## Key Insight

People distrust intentions but accept constraints. A visible process is more credible than verbal assurance.

## How to Apply

For positive outcomes:

- Publish non-negotiable auction rules
    
- Use third-party or platform-governed workflows
    
- Maintain auditable logs of actions and outcomes
    

For negative outcomes:

- Rely on sales promises or personal relationships
    
- Allow discretionary overrides by developers or brokers
    
- Hide decision logic or pricing rationale
    

## Real-World Examples

- Stock exchanges versus OTC deals
    
- Escrow mechanisms in M&A
    
- Smart contracts in financial settlements
    

## Common Pitfalls

- Partial transparency that appears selective
    
- Complex rules that users cannot understand
    
- Exceptions made for “special cases”
