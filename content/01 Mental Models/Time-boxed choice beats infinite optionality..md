## Definition

Decision-making improves when choices are constrained by clear deadlines. Open-ended options prolong evaluation, increase anxiety, and defer commitment.

## Key Insight

Humans do not optimize under unlimited time. They stall. Deadlines force prioritization, tradeoff acceptance, and closure.

## How to Apply

For positive outcomes:

- Use fixed auction windows with immutable close times
    
- Disallow post-deadline negotiations
    
- Tie incentives or penalties to decision windows
    

For negative outcomes:

- Leave listings perpetually open
    
- Signal that better deals may appear later
    
- Allow soft extensions without rule-based triggers
    

## Real-World Examples

- IPO book-building windows
    
- eBay auctions with hard close times
    
- Flash sales in airline and hotel pricing
    

## Common Pitfalls

- Artificial deadlines without enforcement
    
- Frequent deadline extensions that train buyers to wait
    
- Deadlines without sufficient information disclosure
