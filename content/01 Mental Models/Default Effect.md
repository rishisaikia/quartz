## Definition
People tend to stick with pre-selected options. Whatever is chosen as the default becomes disproportionately common because most people don't change it.

## Key Insight
The default option wields enormous power over decisions. Most people accept whatever is pre-selected, even when changing it costs nothing. This isn't laziness—it's how humans make decisions under uncertainty. Whoever controls the default controls the outcome.

## How to Apply
As a decision maker:
- Recognize when you're accepting defaults without thinking
- Question pre-selected options
- Ask "Would I choose this if nothing was pre-selected?"

As a designer:
- Set beneficial defaults for users
- Make the default the option most people should choose
- Understand you're wielding significant influence

## Real-World Example
Organ donation rates: Countries with opt-out defaults (you're a donor unless you decline) have 90%+ donation rates. Countries with opt-in defaults (you must actively choose to donate) have less than 15% donation rates. Same people, same values, different defaults, radically different outcomes.

Retirement savings: When companies auto-enroll employees in 401(k) plans (with opt-out), participation jumps from 40% to 90%. When it's opt-in, most people never sign up. The default determines whether people save for retirement.

Software settings: Most users never change defaults. Software companies know this and set defaults that benefit them (data collection on, privacy features off). Users think they've made choices; actually they've just accepted defaults.

Printer settings default to "Print all pages in color." Most people waste ink printing drafts in color without thinking. If the default was black-and-white, people would save money and only intentionally choose color when needed.

## Common Pitfalls
- Assuming defaults are neutral or optimal (they're neither)
- Abusing default effects to manipulate people into bad choices
- Not recognizing when your own decisions are driven by defaults
- Being paralyzed by every default instead of accepting helpful ones
